# Code of conduct

This is a small technical project. A few rules keep it pleasant and useful.

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** People here ride different bikes, use different tools and
  have different levels of experience. A beginner's capture can settle a question an expert
  could not.
- **Argue with data.** "My capture shows otherwise" is welcome. Say what you measured and how.
  Being wrong about a signal is normal here; several entries in this repository were wrong
  before they were right.
- **Label what you know.** A guess is a hypothesis. Say so, and nobody will mind.
- **Stay in scope.** Reading the bus, yes. Writing to it, defeating safety or emissions
  systems, odometer changes, no. See [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md).
- **No harassment, insults or personal attacks**, in issues, pull requests or discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and, if it continues,
block the account. To raise a concern about someone's conduct, open an issue or contact a
maintainer through their GitHub profile.
